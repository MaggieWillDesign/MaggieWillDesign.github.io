Predictaf

Designing a proactive facility planner for Predictaf an all in one proptech platform.

Project Overview


The Problem

Facility and property operations teams juggle many daily responsibilities, including maintenance requests, vendor coordination, inspections, compliance deadlines, and ongoing facility upkeep. During research, we found that many users—such as property managers, facility managers, and maintenance coordinators—often forget lower-priority tasks, react to incoming alerts instead of planning ahead, and switch between multiple screens to understand priorities across their portfolio of properties or assets.

While the system surfaced individual tasks and alerts, it lacked a central operational view that helped teams quickly understand what required attention today and what was approaching next. As a result, many users operated in a reactive workflow, responding to issues as they appeared rather than proactively planning upcoming maintenance and operational needs.

The Goal

Create an improved planning and daily operations view that helps facility and property operations teams work more proactively through predictive insights and forward-looking planning. The goal was to provide a way for users to quickly identify urgent tasks while also gaining visibility into upcoming deadlines, inspections, and maintenance needs. By surfacing both immediate priorities and upcoming responsibilities in a single view, teams could better plan their day, prioritize work, and stay ahead of operational demands.

This feature was implemented using a new component library I designed as part of the platform’s design system, enabling consistent UI patterns and faster development across the product.

User Research Summary

To gain a deeper understanding of user frustrations, needs, and workflows, we conducted a series of interviews and surveys with property managers. The goal was to uncover how teams currently manage daily operations and identify opportunities to improve how they plan, prioritize tasks, and anticipate upcoming maintenance and deadlines.

Usability Study

Parameters

Study Type:

Moderated usability study

Location:

In Person & Virtual

Participants:

5 participants

Length:

30-40 minutes

Findings

1. Countdown Signaling

Participants expressed a need for more visual indicators for high-level tasks. They wanted to see countdowns or status signals that highlight upcoming deadlines, inspections, or maintenance events, enabling them to plan proactively rather than reactively.

2. Actionable Workflows

Users requested the ability to link high-priority items directly to actionable tickets or workflows. This would allow them to move seamlessly from observing a task to executing the necessary action, reducing friction and context switching.

3. Personal Tasks

Managers wanted a space for personal notes, to-dos, or reminders that aren’t captured in the system data. This allows them to track intuition-based tasks or observations that are unique to their workflow.

4. Intelligent Prioritization

Maintenance teams struggled to separate high-impact work from operational noise. The goal was to help users prioritize tasks, anticipate issues, and make more proactive decisions.

Accessibility Considerations

1.

I meticulously reviewed the product screens to ensure compliance with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) Level AA standards. This involved a thorough examination of various elements such as color contrast, text size, keyboard navigation, and alternative text for images, among others.

2.

Jakarta Sans is the exclusive font family utilized across both platform design and marketing materials. Opting for sans-serif fonts prioritizes readability, ensuring effortless comprehension for users.

3.

I implemented a structured text hierarchy throughout the application, helping users to easily distinguish between different sections and information presented on the screen.

Accessibility Considerations

1_

All interfaces were designed and reviewed against WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility standards, with a focus on color contrast, typography, keyboard navigation, and overall usability to ensure an inclusive experience for all users.

2_

A streamlined typography system utilizing two font families was established across both the platform and marketing materials. Clear distinctions between headings and body text improve readability, consistency, and brand recognition.

3_

A structured text and layout hierarchy was implemented throughout the application to help users quickly scan content, understand priorities, and navigate complex information with confidence.

Going Forward

Takeaways, Next Steps & Feedback

Takeaways

Impact

The independent nature of self-employment often leads individuals to overlook the importance of retirement planning. Without the structure of employer-sponsored retirement programs or guidance from HR departments, many freelancers neglect this critical aspect of financial planning. However, adopting a proactive approach to retirement investing not only secures long-term financial stability but also empowers individuals to take control of their financial futures. The value of the platform lies in its ability to empower numerous users to seize retirement investment opportunities and leverage the benefits of compound interest and tax-deferred growth, thereby enabling them to build substantial retirement funds.

What I learned

In reflecting on my experiences with this case study, I've garnered valuable insights and expertise. Here are some key takeaways:

  • Recognizing the intricate design requirements for financial platforms

  • Understanding the potential impacts of AI on product efficacy, both beneficial and detrimental

  • The nuances of designing for dark mode

  • Prioritizing the user perspective to optimize outcomes

Next Steps

1.

For an improved design, solicit input on user experience (UX) and user interface (UI) elements from colleagues specializing in the field. Actively involve peers with expertise in UX/UI design, urging them to offer constructive feedback on the existing design.

2.

Enhance the social aspects, fostering a sense of community where users can connect, share their experiences, and motivate each other on their fitness journeys. Building a supportive environment is integral to the vision.

3.

Focus on expanding accessibility features and brand voice, ensuring the app is usable and beneficial to individuals of varying abilities and needs.
